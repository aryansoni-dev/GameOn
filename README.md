# GameOn

**GameOn** is a web application that allows users to **find nearby sports academies, turfs, gyms, and fitness hubs** and easily **book slots, purchase memberships, and manage their activities**.  
It bridges the gap between sports enthusiasts and facility providers by offering a seamless booking and management experience.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔎 **Search & Discover** – Locate nearby turfs, gyms, and sports academies using map-based search.  
- ⏰ **Real-time Booking** – View available slots and book instantly.  
- 💳 **Memberships & Payments** – Buy memberships and pay securely online.  
- 🗓 **Provider Dashboard** – Facility owners can manage slots, pricing, and memberships.  
- 📊 **User Dashboard** – Users can manage bookings, memberships, and favorites.  
- ⭐ **Reviews & Ratings** – Share feedback and rate facilities.  
- 🔔 **Notifications** – Get email/SMS reminders for bookings and memberships.
